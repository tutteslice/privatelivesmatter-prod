Browser Fingerprinting

You are tracked not by cookies, but by your unique screen resolution, fonts, and hardware.

Mechanism: Scripts query your browser's canvas rendering and audio context to build a unique hash.

Countermeasure: Use Firefox (Hardened) or Brave Browser which randomize these values.

👁️
EXPAND +

Surveillance Capitalism

"Free" services monetize your behavioral data, predicting your actions better than you can.

Mechanism: Real-time bidding (RTB) auctions your profile to advertisers in milliseconds.

Countermeasure: Block 3rd-party scripts (uBlock Origin) and avoid SSO (Log in with Google/Facebook).

📍
EXPAND +

Metadata Aggregation

It's not just what you say, but who you talk to, when, and from where.

Mechanism: ISPs and mobile carriers log DNS requests and tower connections.

Countermeasure: Use Encrypted DNS (DoH) and a reputable VPN to decouple location from identity.

Behavioral Protocols

Technology alone cannot save you. Your habits are the weak link. Toggle the tabs below to see how to shift your behavior.

🚫 The Vulnerable Habit

  • Using Chrome logged into a Google Account.
  • Accepting "All Cookies" to remove popups quickly.
  • Searching for sensitive medical/financial info on standard engines.
  • Keeping 50 tabs open, leaving session tokens active.

✅ The Secure Protocol

  • Compartmentalization: Use different browsers for different tasks (e.g., Firefox for social, Brave for searching).
  • Search Agnosticism: Use DuckDuckGo or StartPage to decouple search queries from your ID.
  • Cookie Autodelete: Use extensions that wipe cookies when a tab closes.

🚫 The Vulnerable Habit

  • Posting real-time location photos.
  • Participating in "Viral Quizzes" (e.g., "What was your first car?")—these are security question harvesting.
  • Using "Sign in with Facebook" on 3rd party apps.

✅ The Secure Protocol

  • Disinformation: Populate profiles with slightly incorrect data (wrong birthdate) to poison the data well.
  • Time-Shifting: Post photos days after the event, never while you are still at the location.
  • Burner Accounts: Use unique emails for every social platform.

🚫 The Vulnerable Habit

  • Leaving WiFi and Bluetooth on when leaving the house.
  • Granting "Always Allow" location access to retail apps.
  • Using unencrypted SMS for sensitive conversations.

✅ The Secure Protocol

  • Radio Silence: Toggle "Airplane Mode" or turn off WiFi/BT when commuting to prevent retail tracking beacons.
  • Signal Protocol: Move all text communication to Signal or Session apps.
  • App Audit: Regularly revoke permissions for microphone and location in settings.
